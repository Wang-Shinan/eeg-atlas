EEG Atlas — electrode templates and anatomical surfaces

This viewer is for education, not individual source localization or diagnosis.

ELECTRODES
MNE-Python built-in montage tables, exported with make_standard_montage and
compute_native_head_t. The JSON records the exact version, original coordinates,
coordinate frame, transformation, and SHA-256 source hashes. Default MNE head
sizes are retained. This is not participant digitization or a manufacturer
certification of an individual's cap placement.
Source: https://mne.tools/stable/auto_tutorials/intro/40_sensor_locations.html
MNE license: https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-python/blob/main/LICENSE.txt

ANATOMICAL SURFACES
FreeSurfer fsaverage5 pial surfaces, distributed with Nilearn 0.12.1, and the
fsaverage head surface and MRI/head transformation distributed with MNE-Python.
Nilearn's fsaverage5 description does not supply a separate dataset license;
the FreeSurfer data license below is retained, not replaced by Nilearn's
software license. Attribution: FreeSurfer / The General Hospital Corporation;
Fischl et al., 1999, High-resolution intersubject averaging and a coordinate
system for the cortical surface. https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.1055
Sources:
https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/fswiki/FsAverage
https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/tree/0.12.1/nilearn/datasets/data/fsaverage5
https://github.com/freesurfer/freesurfer/blob/dev/LICENSE.txt

MODIFICATIONS
These are derived representations, NOT unmodified upstream files: coordinates
converted from mm to m as needed, transformed from MRI surface RAS or montage
native coordinates into MNE head coordinates, rounded and serialized to JSON.
Mesh topology is preserved. No channel jitter, surface projection, mesh
inflation, individual registration, or renderer-side anatomical scaling is
applied. Package versions and input hashes are in the downloadable JSON.
